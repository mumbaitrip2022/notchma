# Code of Conduct

NotchBar should be a friendly place to ask, report and build, whoever you are and however much experience you have.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use [a private report](https://github.com/mumbaitrip2022/notchma/security/advisories/new). Only the maintainer can see it.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
